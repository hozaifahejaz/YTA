# Script review

Check, in this order:

1. **Hook (first 15 seconds).** Does it open on the most interesting moment or question? Cut greetings, channel plugs and "in this video".
2. **Promise and payoff.** The title's promise is answered, and not only in the last minute.
3. **Momentum.** Every section raises a new question or answers one. Cut paragraphs that only summarise.
4. **Accuracy.** Every number, date and name matches the fact sheet. Mark anything unsupported.
5. **Voice.** Reads like a person talking: varied sentence length, concrete words, no stock phrases ("delve", "tapestry", "in today's fast-paced world", "it's important to note", "buckle up").
6. **Length.** Within the target word count.
7. **Policy.** No misleading claims, no unlicensed material described as shown, no repetitive template wording.

Return specific edits with the reason for each, not general advice.
