# Thumbnail concepts

- One idea per thumbnail: a single subject, readable at phone size.
- Three words or fewer of text, never repeating the title.
- Strong contrast between subject and background; follow the channel's colours from `styles/thumbnail.json` and `brand/brand.json`.
- Create curiosity the video pays off. Never promise what the video does not show.
- For a faceless channel, the subject is an object, place, document or diagram, shown big and close.

For each concept give: the main image, the text (if any), the layout, and why a viewer would click.
